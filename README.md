# 🛡️ TrustMart AI

**Buy From People You Can Trust.**

TrustMart AI is a safe and trusted online marketplace for **clothes, shoes, and jewellery**.

Here, you do not just buy and sell. You buy and sell with people who have been verified.

> Every day, people lose money to fake sellers and fake buyers on Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook.
> TrustMart was built to solve this problem.

**Live Demo:** https://trustmart-ai.vercel.app
**Stack:** HTML + Tailwind CSS + GSAP + Groq AI + Supabase + Vercel

## ✨ What Is TrustMart?

TrustMart AI helps you:

- Create your account with AI
- Add your goods
- Find customers
- Check if the person you want to do business with is legit — **before you send your money**

### For Sellers
Add your goods, get verified, get recommended, find clients.

### For Buyers
Search clothes, shoes, and jewellery, check any seller before you pay, and buy with confidence.

### For Everyone
Ask TrustMart AI at any time — *“Is this person legit?”* — and the AI will verify and tell you everything about that person.

---

## 🧠 How Does Trust Work?

Trust on TrustMart works in three simple steps:

### 1. Verify
We verify your phone number, address, NIN, and identity.

### 2. Score
We give you a Trust Score from **0 to 100** based on your verification, sales, reviews, and reports.

### 3. Recommend
If your score is high, TrustMart recommends you to buyers and sellers as **Trusted and Legit**. If your score is low, we do not recommend you.

> The higher your Trust Score, the more customers trust you.
