What Is TrustMart All About?

TrustMart is a safe and trusted online marketplace for clothes, shoes, and jewellery. Here, you do not just buy and sell. You buy and sell with people who have been verified. TrustMart AI helps you create your account, add your goods, find customers, and check if the person you want to do business with is legit – before you send your money.

Why Do You Need Trust?

Every day, people lose money to fake sellers and fake buyers on Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook. A seller collects money and never delivers. A buyer orders goods and never pays. You need Trust because without trust, there is no safe business. TrustMart was built to solve this problem.

How Does Trust Work?

1. Verify – We verify your phone number, address, NIN, and identity.
2. Score – We give you a Trust Score from 0 to 100 based on your verification, sales, reviews, and reports.
3. Recommend – If your score is high, TrustMart recommends you to buyers and sellers as Trusted and Legit. If your score is low, we do not recommend you. The higher your Trust Score, the more customers trust you.
