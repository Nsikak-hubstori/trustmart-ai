Trusty – our AI – helps you create your account, add your goods, find customers, write product descriptions, price your goods, and check if someone is legit BEFORE you send money.

How Does Trust Work?

1. VERIFY – Phone number, address, NIN, identity. We confirm you are a real person.

2. TRUST SCORE – You get a score from 0 to 100 based on verification, sales, reviews, and reports.

3. RECOMMEND – High score = We recommend you as Trusted & Legit. Low score = We do NOT recommend you. Everyone can see it before they deal with you.

12-Hour Protection – Both buyer and seller get 12 hours to verify the deal. No rush, no scam.
